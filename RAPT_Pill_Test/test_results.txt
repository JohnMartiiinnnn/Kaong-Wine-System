RAPT Pill BLE Test Results
==========================
Date: 2026-06-13
Hardware: Primary ESP32 (temporarily flashed with RAPT_Pill_Test firmware)
Medium: Tap water (room temperature)


--- PHASE 1: Raw Readings (No Gravity Offset) ---

Firmware: Initial version with GRAVITY_OFFSET not yet applied.
The RAPT Pill was submerged in tap water. Expected SG on water = 1.0000.
Observed readings were consistently below 1.000, indicating a calibration offset is needed.

SG: 0.9910  Temp: 31.25C  Batt: 100%  RSSI: -77 dBm
SG: 0.9912  Temp: 31.12C  Batt: 100%  RSSI: -69 dBm
SG: 0.9911  Temp: 30.94C  Batt: 100%  RSSI: -73 dBm

Average raw SG on water: ~0.9911
Offset required: 1.0000 - 0.9911 = 0.0089, rounded to 0.009


--- PHASE 2: Calibration Applied (GRAVITY_OFFSET = 0.009) ---

Firmware updated to apply GRAVITY_OFFSET = 0.009f to each decoded SG reading.
Same test conditions: RAPT Pill submerged in tap water.

SG: 1.0002  Temp: 30.75C  Batt: 100%  RSSI: -67 dBm
SG: 1.0002  Temp: 30.62C  Batt: 100%  RSSI: -69 dBm
SG: 0.9990  Temp: 30.56C  Batt: 100%  RSSI: -78 dBm
SG: 0.9996  Temp: 30.62C  Batt: 100%  RSSI: -84 dBm
SG: 0.9996  Temp: 30.62C  Batt: 100%  RSSI: -89 dBm

Calibrated readings are now within +/- 0.001 of 1.0000 on water. Acceptable.


--- NOTES ---

Temperature (~30-31C):
  The RAPT Pill sensor measures liquid temperature, not ambient air.
  Tap water sitting out or slight self-heating from the pill's BLE radio
  accounts for the reading being above room temperature. This is normal.

RSSI variation (-67 to -89 dBm):
  Signal strength fluctuates naturally due to BLE advertising intervals,
  multipath interference, and the pill's orientation in water. This does
  not affect gravity or temperature accuracy.

Duplicate suppression:
  An earlier firmware version printed every BLE advertisement, flooding
  the serial monitor. The final firmware only prints when the SG value
  changes, keeping output clean and readable.

Serial garbage on monitor open:
  The ESP32 boot ROM outputs at 74880 baud. Opening the monitor at 115200
  causes the initial boot log to appear as garbage characters. This is
  normal and stops once the application starts.


--- CALIBRATION APPLIED TO MAIN FIRMWARE ---

GRAVITY_OFFSET = 0.009 is defined in src/config.h and applied in
src/main.cpp at the UART receive point (after Secondary ESP32 transmits
raw pill data). All downstream systems (display, logging, web UI, ABV
calculation) use the corrected value automatically.
